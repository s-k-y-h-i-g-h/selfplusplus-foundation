Our Mission

The self++ Foundation is a registered UK charity dedicated to advancing human health and longevity. We believe that life-extending treatments and supplements should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their economic status.

Through controlled access to Antarctic resources, we fund research, distribute treatments at cost, and educate the public on evidence-based longevity interventions.
