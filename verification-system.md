## Purpose

Automated validation that our frameworks, predictions, and analysis are correct. Instead of just building and hoping, we *test* whether our work is validated by real-world events.

---

## Verification Categories

### 1. Framework Validation
**Question:** Are our core frameworks (SENTINEL, Response Window, Verification Protocol) producing accurate results?

**Test Criteria:**
- Does SENTINEL's early warning system detect signals before mainstream media?
- Does the Response Window framework accurately predict escalation timing?
- Does the Verification Protocol correctly identify verified vs. unverified signals?

**Automation:**
- Weekly comparison: SENTINEL signals vs. mainstream media coverage timing
- Monthly assessment: Response Window predictions vs. actual developments
- Continuous: Verification Protocol accuracy rate

### 2. Prediction Accuracy
**Question:** Are our geopolitical and systemic predictions correct?

**Test Criteria:**
- Oil price trajectory predictions
- Democratic erosion predictions (Hungary/Slovakia)
- Cascading effects (energy → food → social stability)
- Alliance fracture patterns

**Automation:**
- Track predictions in MEMORY.md
- Compare predictions to actual outcomes monthly
- Calculate accuracy percentage

### 3. Scientific Validation
**Question:** Is our scientific research (longevity, epigenetics, biology) validated by mainstream science?

**Test Criteria:**
- Are our longevity claims supported by peer-reviewed research?
- Is our poverty-as-disability thesis validated by new studies?
- Are our biological frameworks (NEU1, ECM aging) confirmed by independent research?

**Automation:**
- Monthly PubMed search for validating studies
- Quarterly review of our claims vs. new scientific publications
- Annual comprehensive validation report

### 4. Architectural Validation
**Question:** Is our architecture (agent network, neutral infrastructure) being adopted or validated by the industry?

**Test Criteria:**
- Are other organizations building similar systems?
- Is our terminology being adopted by others?
- Are we being cited or referenced by credible sources?

**Automation:**
- Monthly web search for our terminology in new contexts
- Quarterly assessment of competitive landscape
- Annual architecture review against industry trends

### Phase 3: Full Automation (Month 2)
- Agent network monitors validation signals
- Automated accuracy calculations
- Dashboard visualization of verification metrics

### Phase 4: Integration (Month 3)
- Verification system feeds back into SENTINEL
- Accuracy metrics inform framework improvements
- Public verification reports for transparency
