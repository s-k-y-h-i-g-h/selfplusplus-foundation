## The Core Insight

**Humanity's systemic failure mirrors biological death.**

When a human body dies, it's not one organ that fails. It's a cascade:
- Heart stops → blood stops flowing → organs fail → brain dies
- Each failure triggers the next
- The cascade is what kills, not the initial failure

**The same pattern is happening to human civilization:**
- Energy system fails (oil shock) → food system fails → social stability fails → governance fails → civilization dies
- Each failure triggers the next
- The cascade is what kills, not the initial failure

## The Biological Parallel

### How a Body Dies

| Stage | Body | Civilization |
|-------|------|--------------|
| **Trigger** | Heart attack or organ failure | Energy shock (Strait of Hormuz closure) |
| **Cascade** | Blood stops → organs fail | Oil prices spike → food prices spike → social unrest |
| **Collapse** | Brain death | Institutional collapse |
| **Result** | Biological death | Civilizational death |

### The Cascade Mechanics

**In the body:**
1. Heart stops pumping
2. Blood stops delivering oxygen
3. Organs begin to fail (kidneys, liver, brain)
4. Immune system can't compensate
5. Multiple organ failure
6. Death

**In civilization:**
1. Energy supply disrupted
2. Transport and food production affected
3. Prices spike, households struggle
4. Governments can't respond effectively
5. Social unrest, institutional decay
6. Systemic collapse

## The Intervention Window

### In Medicine: The Golden Hour

The first hour after a critical injury is the "golden hour" — intervention during this window dramatically increases survival.

- Catch the heart attack early → survival
- Miss the window → cascading failure → death

### In Civilization: The Response Window

The time between detecting systemic failure and the cascade reaching critical mass is the "Response Window."

- **Detection Lead Time** — How early we detect the signal
- **Deployment Lead Time** — How fast we can intervene
- **Response Window** — Detection minus Deployment

**Positive Response Window = Preemption Possible**
**Negative Response Window = Too Late**

## The Homeostasis Connection

### Biological Homeostasis

The body maintains balance through:
- **Sensors** — detect deviations from normal
- **Effectors** — initiate corrective actions
- **Feedback loops** — restore equilibrium

### Civilizational Homeostasis

Civilization maintains balance through:
- **Sensors** — media, institutions, monitoring systems
- **Effectors** — governments, organizations, communities
- **Feedback loops** — elections, markets, social movements

**When homeostasis fails:**
- Body: fever, infection, organ failure
- Civilization: inflation, unrest, institutional collapse

**SENTINEL is civilizational homeostasis.** It detects deviations, enables effectors, and restores balance.
